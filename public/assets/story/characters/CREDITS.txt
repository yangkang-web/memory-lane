CROWD
infected-crowd.glb derives from the same Rosswet Mobile CC BY 3.0 source above.
Original low-detail mesh retained (3,340 triangles); 24 local skeletal poses baked
at load time and rendered as instances, with deterministic size/color/phase variation.
No third-party animations included. Routes and the 60-second edit are unchanged.
Mirror hands additionally use DJMaesen's FPS arms skin (CC BY 4.0 above),
filtered to the glove/hand triangles; original survivor hand triangles are hidden.

Delivered derivative: infected-detailed.glb. Local 19-bone rig and smooth skin weights,

thicker legs and impact-settling morph. Original UVs and textures are retained.
Fungal growths and landing choreography are locally authored.
